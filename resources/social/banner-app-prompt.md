## App screenshot provenance

`bridgeclip-app-demo.png` is a browser screenshot of the repository's existing BridgeClip website twin at `bridgeclip-website/public/twin/`. The twin uses the app's React renderer with bundled demonstration content; it is not a screenshot of the user's private jobs or live Electron session. The captured screen is the completed clip results for the bundled Build Log podcast demo. Imagegen used that real interface screenshot as the visual insert in the final composition. The final banner is generated artwork based on the screenshot, rather than a pixel-identical screenshot reproduction.
